Dramatic Skill Shift

Level Up: From TIME to PLACE Openers!

You mastered setting the stage with Time. Now switch your opener from a clock to a compass—start your sentence with a prepositional phrase of Place!

TIME (Old) → PLACE + APPOSITIVE (New!)
Model 1 • Place Opener + Appositive + Participial Phrase
Choppy: Clara discovered a brass key beneath the library floorboards. Clara is a curious archivist. She lifted the heavy oak plank. She examined the engraved lock.
Beneath the library floorboards, Clara, a curious archivist, discovered a brass key, examining the engraved lock.
Model 2 • Place Opener + Appositive + Participial Phrase
Choppy: Julian pruned the rare orchids inside the misty glass conservatory. Julian is the head botanist. He used silver shears. He misted their delicate leaves.
Inside the misty glass conservatory, Julian, the head botanist, pruned the rare orchids, misting their delicate leaves.
